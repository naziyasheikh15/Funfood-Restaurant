About CodeHelp

CodeHelp is a leading Indian tech education platform founded by Love Babbar and Lakshay Kumar, designed to help students and working professionals secure engineering roles at top product-based companies. The platform is highly regarded for its comprehensive tech bootcamps, which cover advanced Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA), full-stack web development using the MERN stack, and system design patterns. CodeHelp distinguishes itself through its centralized learning ecosystem, offering students built-in online compilers, active Discord peer communities, mock test series, and automated AI tools for code reviewing and instant doubt solving. In addition to premium structured batches, the platform provides expansive free career roadmaps and extensive programming playlists on the CodeHelp YouTube channel, making high-quality tech placement preparation accessible to millions of aspiring software developers.
