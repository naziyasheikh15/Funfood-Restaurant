TRIBUTE TO GULZAR SAHAB

Gulzar sahab (born Sampooran Singh Kalra) is a legendary Indian poet, lyricist, and filmmaker. He is globally celebrated for revolutionizing Hindi cinema with deeply evocative, simple, and profound song lyrics, creating a new genre of three-line poetry called Triveni, and directing sensitive, critically acclaimed films like Aandhi and Mausam
